# Dark factory baseline

- Open **draft** PRs. Never merge, never enable Autopilot — Riddler proof →
  Gordon review → human plate.
- One verifiable unit per PR; state **Done means** (falsifiable exit) and
  **Keep** (invariants) in the PR body and in every handoff.
- `Dahhrk/dark-factory` is the public kitchen: recipes only, no product
  Feature Maps, no secrets.
- Hitting a spend, quota, or credential wall means stop and say so — do not
  buy, improvise keys, or unlock overnight fleets.
